For schools & small businesses

Tools that work in a real school and a real shop

Hadtah apps are free, work on low-cost phones and shared computers, and most need no internet once installed. Here is what fits, and how to set it up.

🏫 Schools

Run the library without paper registers, help students focus during prep, and give learners a safe place to research and revise.

🛒 Small businesses

Record money in and out, know your real profit each month, print reports for a SACCO or bank, and check suspicious Mobile Money messages.

👨‍👩‍👧 Families & students

Track household money, keep children focused while studying, check scam messages, and find clear answers for homework.

Setting up many devices with no internet

  1. On any computer with internet, download the Android APK and Windows installer for the apps you need.
  2. Copy the files to a flash drive.
  3. On each computer, run the Windows installer. On each phone, copy the APK and open it (see FAQ).
  4. The apps then work with no internet at all.

Looking after your records

  • Each device keeps its own records. Choose one computer as the main library or accounts computer.
  • Use Settings → Download backup regularly and keep the file on a flash drive.
  • To move to a new computer, install the app and restore the backup file.

Which app for which job?

  • Offline Library: catalogue, lending, returns, overdue books and PDF reports.
  • Cash Flow: daily takings, expenses, budgets, profit and monthly reports, in UGX or another currency.
  • Ennun: focus timer for revision and work, with distraction blocking on Windows.
  • Lamma: check SMS, WhatsApp and email messages for scams before you act.
  • Eilpah: lessons from verified teachers, following the Ugandan curriculum.
  • TTu: ask a question and get a clear answer with sources (needs internet).
