Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Hadtah makes apps for people in Uganda and across Africa. This page explains, in plain words, what information our website and apps use and why.

This website

The Hadtah website has no ads, no tracking scripts and no sign-up. Your light or dark mode choice is saved in your own browser. The contact form does not send anything from the website; it opens your own email app with the message ready for you to send.

Apps that keep everything on your device

Ennun, Lamma, Offline Library and Cash Flow save what you type (sessions, checked messages, books, members, money records) only on your own phone or computer. We do not receive it. If you delete the app or clear the browser data, it is gone, so use each app’s backup option to keep a copy. Lamma only checks messages you paste in; it never reads your messages by itself.

Online services: TTu and Eilpah

TTu needs your Gmail address to sign you in and keeps your questions and usage so it can answer and apply daily limits. Questions are sent to search and AI services to write the answer. See TTu’s privacy policy for details.

Eilpah keeps the account and learning information needed to run lessons and classes. See the privacy page inside Eilpah.

Ask TTu

The Ask TTu button opens TTu inside a panel. Nothing is shared with TTu until you use it, and TTu’s own privacy policy applies inside the panel.

We never

  • sell your information,
  • show you ads based on your information,
  • read the records you keep in our offline apps.

Children

Ennun, Lamma, Offline Library and Cash Flow can be used by anyone. TTu is for people aged 18 or older, as stated on its sign-in page.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@hadtah.com
